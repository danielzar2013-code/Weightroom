Settings

Video, 3D and speech run on free Hugging Face GPUs. Without a token each device gets a couple of GPU minutes a day. A free account token adds a few more, and a PRO account about 8 times more. The token stays on this device and is only sent to Hugging Face. A Read token is enough.

What runs for real

  1. Images with FLUX on Pollinations (no account) or Z-Image Turbo on Hugging Face.
  2. Video with MiniMax H3, 3D with Hunyuan3D 2.1, and speech in 23 languages, Hebrew included, with Chatterbox. All on Hugging Face.
  3. Chat with gpt-oss-20b on Pollinations (no account).

Your own GPU

The Your GPU mode shows how each model would run on your card. Connecting a GPU server comes next; until then those jobs are a preview.
